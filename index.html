Controls

Field (shooter)

  • WASD move · Shift sprint · Mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB aim/block
  • 1/2 your two guns · 3 or Q longsword · R reload (stowed guns reload themselves)
  • Hold C arm buckler (parry blows & arrows, even with a gun) · V sledgehammer bash (staggers knights)
  • E capture a wounded officer · T supply drop · G breaching charge
  • Z airstrike · X gunship · F enter/exit SUV
  • Sword: click combo, hold for a heavy blow, sprint + click to lunge

Command (strategy)

  • Tab command view: time slows to 20% · Space pause
  • LMB/drag select · RMB move / attack
  • H hold · A advance · C charge · F follow me · R retreat · G back to AI
  • 1 line · 2 wedge · 3 shield wall · B palisade
  • Controller, field: sticks move/look · LT aim/block · RT fire/swing
  • LB hold buckler · RB next weapon · B sword ⇄ gun · R3 sledgehammer
  • X reload · Y capture / enter SUV · hold A or L3 sprint
  • D-pad ◀ airstrike · ▶ gunship · ▲ C4 · ▼ supply drop
  • Controller, command: View switch · Menu pause · left stick pan · right stick cursor
  • A select · B order · X hold · Y charge · ▲ advance · ▼ retreat
  • LB/RB cycle squads · L3 select all · R3 next formation · LT/RT zoom
