Operation KINGSLAYER · Insertion: Shropshire, 21 July 1403

KINGSLAYERS

In 2031 a collapse cascade traced back through six centuries of history converged on one morning: the Battle of Shrewsbury. Harry "Hotspur" Percy rides against King Henry IV with the Welsh rebel Owain Glyndŵr's army still days away. History says Hotspur dies here and the rebellion withers.

You have been sent back with everything the chrono-lift could carry. Keep Hotspur alive, break the royal army in the field, then storm the castle where King Henry waits, and kill him. Hold out long enough and Glyndŵr may yet arrive.

Field (shooter)

  • WASD move · Shift sprint · Mouse aim
  • LMB fire · RMB aim down sights · R reload
  • 1 rifle · 2 shotgun · 3 launcher · 4/Q longsword
  • Sword: click combo (3rd hit spins) · hold to charge a heavy blow · sprint + click to lunge · RMB block
  • G breaching charge (C4) · Z airstrike · X gunship
  • F enter / exit the armoured SUV — stand next to it to resupply

Command (strategy)

  • Tab switch to command view (time slows)
  • LMB / drag select your regiments
  • RMB ground: move · RMB enemy: attack
  • H hold · A advance · R retreat · G hand back to AI
  • B palisade · Space tactical pause · WASD/wheel camera
